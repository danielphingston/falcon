FDD Tracker

A personal work tracker for financial due diligence

FDD Tracker is a free, open-source app for a chartered accountant working on financial due diligence. It keeps engagements, work papers, review points, information request lists (IRLs), follow-ups and reminders in one place. It runs in the browser, works offline and can be installed on a phone or laptop.

Open FDD Tracker

What it does

  • TodayWhat's due, who to chase and today's calendar at a glance.
  • EngagementsWork papers, review points and IRLs for each deal.
  • Follow-upsTrack what you're waiting on from clients and the team.
  • Time and reportsFocus timer, timesheet and daily or weekly summaries.
  • AssistantAsk Gemini to add tasks, log chases or turn a client email into IRL items.
  • Works offlineEverything is saved on your device first.

How it uses your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. The app works on one device without it. If you connect, it asks for:

  • Your email address, to show which account is connected.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to sync your data between your devices. The app can't see your other Drive files.
  • Your Google Calendar events (calendar.events), to add your deadlines, engagement key dates, reminders and focus blocks to your calendar, and to show your calendar next to your work on the Today and Calendar screens. The app only changes or removes events it created itself.
  • Gemini (generative-language.retriever), for the assistant. When you chat with it, your messages, attachments and the app data it looks up are sent to Google's Gemini API.

There is no FDD Tracker server: your data stays on your devices and in your own Google account. Read the privacy policy for the details.

FDD Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
